Jannick.Me

My own website. Something I dreamt of as a kid, just because it sounded so special and complex that it could only be a dream sometime.

As I started studying computer science I quickly learned what a website really is and how "unspecial" it is. The magic was gone. Nethertheless I started writing my own website, after 4 semester, only because I felt it could be a good way to protocol my workflow but also could express myself and encourage myself to finish something I've started.

Vision and Focus

My vision is a really simple website, somewhat extending my GitHub. I want to present projects that can be seen on my GitHub profile but also telling the story behind the project explaining what problems I encountered and what I learned on the way.

That is also the focus, keep it simple, learn something on the way and express yourself.
